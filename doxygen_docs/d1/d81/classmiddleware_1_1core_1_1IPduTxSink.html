Detailed Description

Platform-provided sink that accepts the outbound PDUs of the PDU broker connection.

Only the transmit direction goes through this interface; PDUs received from the bus are handed to the connection with onReceive(). PDUs are handed over in commit order. Runtime dispatch is used because the concrete bus driver is only known at integration time; calls happen at the bus frame rate, not on a hot path.

Hands a PDU over to the sink.

The sink takes over the PDU. It owns the pending transmission, its backlog and what happens when the PDU cannot be sent right away (queueing, retry, acknowledgement, drop policy), and it is responsible for making drops observable. The connection never retries and never holds a PDU back, so a slow or failing destination cannot block the PDUs behind it.

Parameters
idPDU ID.
payloadsnapshot of the PDU, backed by the connection's reusable scratch buffer. It is only valid during the call, so the sink must consume or copy it before returning.
